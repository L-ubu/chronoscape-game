A D&D engine where the DM is an AI.

Multiplayer 5e with an AI Dungeon Master, set in the Chronoscape: World of Aethermere — a cyberpunk-fantasy realm where draconic magic meets bleeding-edge technology.

DM AI, streaming multiplayer Socket.IO clients web + terminal lore Obsidian vault

01Narrative in, game state out

The AI DM streams story with hidden mechanical tags — damage, healing, loot, conditions, XP — and the engine applies them automatically. Flavor and rules, one stream.

chronoscape — party of 3 
KAEL: I vault the bar and hurl my arc grenade at the drake.

/roll 1d20+7 → 18 hit!

DM: The grenade arcs through neon haze and bursts against the
drake's scales in a lattice of blue lightning. It shrieks,
wings clipping a holo-sign that crashes down in sparks.

  [DAMAGE 24 lightning] [CONDITION: stunned, 1 round]
  [XP +150]

  ✓ combat tracker updated · initiative passes to SERAPHINE

02What you get

◆ AI Dungeon Master

Streams narrative with mechanical tags that update game state — damage, healing, loot, conditions, combat, XP.

◆ Real multiplayer

Real-time sessions over Socket.IO. Your whole party in one world, from browsers and terminals alike.

◆ Obsidian-powered lore

Reads your campaign vault — NPCs, bestiary, factions, locations, items, random tables — and feeds ~14k tokens of world context to the DM.

◆ Full 5e mechanics

Dice with advantage and modifiers, combat tracker with initiative, inventory, rests, leveling, lookups.

◆ Two clients

A browser UI for the map-and-sheet crowd, and a terminal client for the ones who live in a shell.

◆ Character creation

AI-guided conversational creation, or a manual 5-step flow with live class previews.

03Commands

Anything without a / goes straight to the DM as a player action.

CommandWhat it does
/roll XdYRoll dice — advantage, disadvantage, modifiers
/sheetView your character sheet
/inventoryView and manage items
/rest short|longTake a rest
/combatView combat state and initiative
/mapShow the world map
/lookup npc|location|faction <name>Query the vault's lore

04Why it exists

The decisions worth knowing about, written down.

The DM never cancels

Scheduling kills more campaigns than dragons do. An AI DM means the party plays whenever three people are free.

The vault is the source of truth

World lore lives in Obsidian where it's pleasant to write — the engine reads it, so the DM actually knows your NPC's name.

Mechanics stay honest

The AI narrates; the engine calculates. Tags keep hit points, conditions and XP exact instead of vibes-based.
